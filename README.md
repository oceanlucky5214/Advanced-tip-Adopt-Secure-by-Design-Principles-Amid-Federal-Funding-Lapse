Attention developers and maintainers: The recent lapse in federal funding has led to reduced oversight of software development practices. To ensure your projects remain secure, consider adopting secure-by-design principles as recommended by CISA and the Biden-Harris Administration. By taking ownership of security outcomes, embracing transparency, and structuring your team for accountability, you can help mitigate risks and align with federal standards. Start by reviewing the [secure software development attestation form](https://go.dhs.gov/lapse-2026) for guidance. Collaboration between the private sector and government is key to fostering a more secure digital ecosystem.
